Watch on TV

Use this phone as your TV remote

Works with Vizio SmartCast TVs on the same Wi‑Fi as this phone. It’s off until you set it up.

  • Arrows, OK, Back, play and volume for your TV.
  • Opens VIPTV on the TV in one tap.
  • What’s playing on this phone stays here. Nothing is sent to the TV.

Your phone will ask to find devices on your local network. VIPTV only uses this to find your TV.
