Components — Phone

Buttons, chips, badges, inputs and selection at 1:1. No focus ring on phone; pressed replaces hover.

Buttons

One accent primary per screen. Pill rule: radius = height ÷ 2. Pressed scales to 0.97.

Chips and segmented controls

Selected chip: surface-3 + 1px 0.22 border. Selected segment: off-white fill.

Badges and indicators

Accent only for progress, spinners and the best-match label. Red only for live.

Inputs

Fields are surface-1 with a hairline; focus raises the border to 0.22 and shows the accent caret.

Selection and settings rows

Every choice list marks the current value with a check and “Current”.
